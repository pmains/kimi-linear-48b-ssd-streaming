Solve the following problem step by step, showing all intermediate reasoning.

A town has two water pumps, A and B. Pump A fills a tank in 6 hours when working
alone. Pump B fills the same tank in 4 hours when working alone. Both pumps are
turned on together at 8:00 AM. At 9:30 AM, pump B is turned off for maintenance.
Pump A continues alone. At 10:15 AM, a valve is opened that drains the tank at a
rate equal to one third of the tank per hour. At 11:00 AM, pump B comes back on.

Questions:
1. At what time does the tank first become full?
2. If instead the drain valve had been opened at the very start, would the tank
   ever fill? If so, when, and what is the earliest time the tank level reaches
   three quarters?

Also generalize: write an expression for the tank level as a piecewise linear
function of time, and explain how you would compute the fill time for arbitrary
pump rates, drain rates, and on/off schedules. Verify your final answers
numerically.
